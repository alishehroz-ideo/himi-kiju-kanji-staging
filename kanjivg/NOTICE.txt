Original SVG files are included unmodified. The core coordinate data in levels.js
is adapted from those outlines: curves were sampled, mapped to an orthogonal grid,
and supplemented with gold connector paths for an Euler-trail puzzle.
Adapted coordinate data is provided under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license.
This puzzle does not enforce standard stroke order. See COPYING.txt for the license.

Character pool: https://www.mext.go.jp/a_menu/shotou/cs/1320015.htm
Archived educational kanji list (1998 curriculum); used as a character pool, not a current grade-alignment claim.

Challenge revision 2: additional edge-disjoint closed auxiliary paths increase
junctions and route-order decisions. All 600 original core glyph outlines and
start/end positions are preserved (translated with the board). Questions are
reordered by the original structural difficulty formula.
